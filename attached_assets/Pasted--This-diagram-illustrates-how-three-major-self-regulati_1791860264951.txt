"""
This diagram illustrates how three major self-regulation frameworks overlap:
- Window of Tolerance (Dan Siegel)
- Polyvagal Theory (Stephen Porges)
- Mindfulness

The center — “Self-Regulation & Clarity” — represents the state where your
nervous system is settled enough to think clearly, feel safely, and act with
intention. It is the place where presence, emotional balance, and flexible
behavior become possible.

THE THREE STATES:
1. Window of Tolerance:
   - Optimal arousal zone where you can stay grounded, responsive, and steady.
   - When inside the window, you can handle stress without becoming overwhelmed
     or shutting down.

2. Polyvagal Theory:
   - Ventral Vagal (Safety & Connection): calm, social, open, regulated.
   - Sympathetic (Fight/Flight): activated, mobilized, anxious, tense.
   - Dorsal Vagal (Shutdown): collapsed, numb, withdrawn, overwhelmed.

3. Mindfulness:
   - Awareness, presence, and the ability to observe your internal state without
     judgment.
   - Helps you notice when you leave the window and gently return.

RETURNING TO REGULATION:
- Slow breathing (especially long exhales)
- Grounding through senses (5–4–3–2–1)
- Gentle movement or stretching
- Orienting to the environment (look around, name what you see)
- Self-talk that is warm, simple, and reassuring
- Connection with a safe person
- Pausing instead of pushing through

BEST PRACTICES FOR WELL-BEING:
- Build daily micro-moments of regulation (short pauses, breaths, check-ins)
- Strengthen ventral vagal tone through connection, rhythm, and breath
- Practice mindfulness in small, doable doses
- Respect your limits and widen your window gradually
- Treat dysregulation as information, not failure

This diagram visually anchors these ideas: regulation is not one skill but the
integration of awareness, physiology, and emotional capacity.
"""

import matplotlib.pyplot as plt
from matplotlib_venn import venn3

plt.figure(figsize=(8,8))
v = venn3(subsets=(1,1,1,1,1,1,1),
          set_labels=('Window of Tolerance', 'Polyvagal Theory', 'Mindfulness'),
          set_colors=('teal', 'lavender', 'lightgray'),
          alpha=0.6)

plt.text(0, 0, 'Self-Regulation & Clarity', ha='center', va='center',
         fontsize=12, fontweight='bold')

plt.annotate('Window of Tolerance = optimal arousal zone',
             xy=v.get_label_by_id('100').get_position(),
             xytext=(-1.5, 1.2),
             fontsize=10,
             arrowprops=dict(arrowstyle='->', color='teal'))

plt.annotate('Polyvagal Theory = nervous system states\n(ventral, sympathetic, dorsal)',
             xy=v.get_label_by_id('010').get_position(),
             xytext=(1.2, 1.2),
             fontsize=10,
             arrowprops=dict(arrowstyle='->', color='purple'))

plt.annotate('Mindfulness = awareness and presence',
             xy=v.get_label_by_id('001').get_position(),
             xytext=(1.2, -1.2),
             fontsize=10,
             arrowprops=dict(arrowstyle='->', color='gray'))

plt.title('Integration of Window of Tolerance, Polyvagal Theory, and Mindfulness',
          fontsize=14)
plt.show()
